TITLE: The road to the flight deck
SUBJECT: Introduction
SUMMARY: What the 13 ATPL theory exams are, how a "frozen ATPL" works, the exam rules you must know, and how to study.
KEY: PPL = fly privately (9 theory exams). CPL = fly for pay. ATPL = airline captain.
KEY: Frozen ATPL = CPL + instrument rating + all 13 ATPL theory exams passed. It unfreezes at 1,500 hours (incl. 500 multi-pilot) plus a skill test.
KEY: Multiple choice, 4 options, no negative marking, 75% pass mark in every subject.
KEY: EASA limits: 18 months, max 6 sittings, max 4 attempts per subject. Break one and you start the whole set again.
KEY: Passes stay valid for 36 months for issuing a CPL or instrument rating.
KEY: You must be enrolled with an approved training organisation (ATO). Get your Class 1 medical first.

K: Not under EASA. You have to be enrolled on a theory course with an approved training organisation, an ATO. The school checks that you've completed the course, and recommends you for the exams. Many schools offer distance learning, so you study at home and go in for some classroom days.
